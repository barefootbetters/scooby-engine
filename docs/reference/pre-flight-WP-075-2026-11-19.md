## After execution — lessons learned

**Lesson: the WP-074 clip-name trap recurs, and the fix is the disc sound index, not the diff.** The numbered scene lines are `Center_of_Maze.250X_`, `Fountain.250D_`, `Well_in_Maze.290_` — **dotted**, and shipping with a speaker suffix (`…250B_1_VELMA`, `…250D_1_NARRATOR`). A first pass probed them with exact `findClip` and the reference agreed on "found 0" while the names were right. Probing the disc's sound index directly (`probe_audio`) showed they ship by prefix, and the check switched to `Sound::hasClipPrefix` for the voice lines (and kept exact `findClip` for the sfx, which ship under their exact id). A 0-diff with "found 0" proves engine == reference, not that either is faithful — the independent disc read is what closes it.

**Lesson: the audit is a starting point; the binary is the source.** The group-7 table tagged `ROOM_P23_Maze_Center` with "opening", but the constructor's 4th argument is the empty string for the centre (and the well) and `Fountain.250D_` for the fountain — the opening is the **fountain's**. The engine's own `room_setup` table already had it right; the audit's column did not. Trusting the trace over the audit avoided porting an opening onto the wrong room.

**Lesson: a second independent trace is cheap insurance against a shared misreading.** Two sessions traced all three classes in Ghidra, independently, and their reads agreed on every clip name, field offset and the split decision. Combined with the engine == reference line-for-line match and the disc sound-index probe, that is three independent authorities, not one — the WP-073/074 anti-shared-misreading move carried up to the session level.

**Scope note:** one fork commit of files staged by name: the three room classes and their check in new files, a factory line, the module entries, the check's declaration and its call, and one line in `maze_rooms.cpp` so the hedge maze reads the centre's `MazeCenterFromGarden` flag (completing WP-074's centre-start link, which the WP-074 check already covered by passing the flag explicitly).

**Pre-existing observation (not this WP):** the WP-069 `arena check pile` level-0 line is non-deterministic between runs — the Brad-room transition sometimes completes within the check's tick budget (`to ROOM_Brad_SPOOKY`) and sometimes does not (`to -`). It is timing/RNG-sensitive and in the regression exclusion list; it flipped between the WP-074 and WP-075 runs. A separate fix for the arena check's level-0 budget would make it deterministic.
