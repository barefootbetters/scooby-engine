## After execution — lessons learned

**Lesson: count a room's sprite classes, not only its own functions.** The audit's 249 instructions were the room's; its game lives in classes its entry builds.

**Lesson: model the tree tick's order when sprites update themselves.** Each sprite's update runs right after its own player, front to back, and a sprite made behind the one updating is ticked in the same walk. The ticks the reference predicted depend on it.

**Lesson: a thrower waits for its own sequence.** Rascal tosses only once his head is still, and its idle replays at random; time a random actor from its own start.
