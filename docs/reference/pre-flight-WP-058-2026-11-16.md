## After execution — lessons learned

**Lesson: rerun before blaming a change for a diff.** The imported clue name flipped between two runs of one build; two earlier WPs had taken the same flip for their own effect.

**Lesson: bound a check's loops by the engine's own constants.** Reading eight toolbar slots of four read past the array and made the check report tokens that weren't there.

**Lesson: a clickable starts on its placement's state,** not on its A.

**Scope note:** one fork commit of 9 files staged by name: the class and its check in new files, `RoomScript::checkOpening()` split out of `setup()`, a factory line, the module entries, the check's declaration and its call.
