## After execution — lessons learned

**Lesson: a character clicked is the class's to take, when the class acts.** Gummy and the donkey are read by their clicked flags in the original's tick. The fork's `RoomClass::click` takes the clicks the class acts on, and lets the inventory answer the rest, such as the wrong item.

**Lesson: a hint counter can move at arrival.** The opening takes Gummy's next hint, so the first click after arriving gives the second.

**Scope note:** one fork commit of 6 files staged by name. Besides the factory line and the module entry, `room.h` gained two getters.
