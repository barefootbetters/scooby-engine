## After execution — lessons learned

**Lesson: detection guards the reader.** A truncated or resized `TGIFILE.ART` fails detection, so only damage beyond the MD5-covered first 5,000 bytes, at the original size, reaches `ArtArchive::open()`. Corruption tests have to keep the detection signature.

**Lesson: under MSYS2, capture engine output with `--logfile`.** A killed `scummvm.exe` loses buffered stdout, and that cut Phantom's first log short. `--logfile` writes lines as they happen. Lines carry a `[timestamp] ` prefix to strip before diffing.

**Lesson: PowerShell variables ignore case.** A loop counter `$t` overwrote the path in `$T` and broke the restore step. Use distinct names.

**Scope note:** the lock held. One fork commit under `engines/scooby/`. The ISOs were mounted read-only and dismounted afterwards. The corrupted copy lived in the scratchpad, was restored and hash-checked against the cache, then deleted.
