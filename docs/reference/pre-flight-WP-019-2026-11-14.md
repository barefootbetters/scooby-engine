## After execution — lessons learned

**Lesson: a known format makes a short WP.** WP-004 had pinned the format, down to the sector rule for the PCM start, so the engine reader was written once and matched all 2,232 clips on its first run.

**Lesson: you can't hear in a headless test, so check what the mixer did.** The mixer's elapsed time, against wall time and against the clip's computed length, shows that the stream was consumed at the right rate. The MD5s show the bytes are right.

**Lesson: scripted keystrokes don't reach a background-launched SDL window.** Neither `SendKeys` nor `keybd_event` opened the debugger. A config key is the reliable way to trigger engine features in tests, and it's worth planning for in each WP's pre-flight.

**Scope note:** the lock held. One fork commit, staged by name. The corrupt-file test used a scratch copy plus hard links, deleted afterwards. The ISOs were dismounted.
