## Doc-vs-tree cross-check

- **The click path** (`0x402130` → `0x404820` → `0x4024C0`) ends in the current room object's vtable `+0x24`. Showdown has 47 room classes. 31 use the default handler `0x40C1E0`; the other 16 override it, and every override calls the default once its story checks pass. This WP implements the default; the overrides are per-room code.
- **`character=` and `direction=`** are in 125 exit sections, but the exit constructor (`0x401850`) never reads them. It reads `fred=`, `daphne=`, `velma=`, `shaggy=` and `scooby=`.
- **WP-019's background** said the transition plays "a voice line". It plays a scene: numbered lines, each spoken by the character whose clip exists, with lip sync.

## Risk review

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Timing depends on audio.** Lines end when their clips end, so a headless check can't replay a scene tick for tick. | high | med | Split the check. The scene's lines are resolved by name (deterministic). The walk-off is a pure tick simulation, checked tick by tick against a reference. Playback and lip sync are checked live. |
| **Story overrides block exits** in the original (Town Center's first exits, for example). | high | low | Out of scope; the default handler runs everywhere until per-room code exists. Documented as a deviation. |
| **The talking sequence is driven by lip-sync bytes**; a shape past the sequence's last step would draw out of range. | low | med | Clamp the step and count how often the data would need it. |
