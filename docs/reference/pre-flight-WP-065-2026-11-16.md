## After execution — lessons learned

**Lesson: probe a character's hit area before scripting a click on it.** Ewan stands behind Fred; the first live click hit Fred. A hover probe found the part of Ewan's step polygon that reaches past him.

**Lesson: a hook for one field of an exit is half of the original's handler.** `+0x24` gets the whole exit; the fork's `leaving` exposed only the scene. The movie now has its own hook.

**Scope note:** one fork commit of 9 files staged by name: the room and its check in new files, the exit-movie hook in `room_script.{h,cpp}`, a factory line, the module entries, the check's declaration and its call.
