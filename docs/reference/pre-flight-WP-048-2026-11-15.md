## After execution — lessons learned

**Lesson: an item used on an exit is the class's to take.** The fork's exits answer only the arrow, and the inventory says "used on nothing" for any other click with an item. `RoomClass::click` runs first, so the post office takes the lantern on the hole there, and the room's tick does the rest.

**Lesson: a room can remember where the gang came from.** `OldRoomName` decides who is here when the gang comes back from somewhere other than the dark tunnel or Street 1, such as the lit tunnel.
